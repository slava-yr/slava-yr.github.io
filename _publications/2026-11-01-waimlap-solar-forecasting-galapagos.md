## Abstract

This study develops an XGBoost-based model for short-term solar irradiance forecasting in the Galapagos Islands. The unique equatorial maritime climate and limited historical data present distinct challenges for renewable energy planning in this ecologically sensitive archipelago. The proposed approach achieves competitive forecasting accuracy while remaining interpretable and computationally lightweight.
