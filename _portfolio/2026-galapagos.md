## Overview

GalapagOS was our entry for the summit-wide hackathon at **SALA '26 — the Summit of AI in LatAm** (March 9–12, 2026, USFQ, Quito, Ecuador), where teams tackle real-world challenges with mentorship from AI researchers and industry experts. We took on solar forecasting for the Galapagos Islands, whose isolated grid and unique equatorial-maritime climate make renewable-energy planning especially hard.

🏆 **2nd place — SALA '26 Hackathon**

Our team actually came together by chance: our flight got stuck in Guayaquil on the way to the summit, and that's where we all met. The delay is why the team ended up calling itself "Guayaquil Stuck Flight."

## Pitching in English for the first time

The part I'll remember most wasn't the model, it was standing up to pitch in English, for the first time, in front of an international audience drawn from across Latin America and beyond. Explaining the problem, the approach, and why it mattered under time pressure, in a second language, to a room of researchers and peers, was well outside my comfort zone. Placing 2nd made it all worth it.

## Where it led

The idea we prototyped here became the seed for a full study on short-term solar forecasting for the Galapagos Islands, later written up as a paper.
