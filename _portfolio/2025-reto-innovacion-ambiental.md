## Overview

We pitched a portable kit that lets cyclists generate energy on the go using a dynamo and a portable charger. Once charged, the portable batteries are dropped off at larger charge points around campus, where they become available for any student to borrow. An integrated app ties it together, offering benefits and incentives to students who take part.

The goal is to encourage sustainable transportation while giving students a practical way to keep their devices charged.

The challenge, organized by [CIDE-PUCP](https://emprende.pucp.edu.pe/reto/innovacion-ambiental/) (Centro de Innovación y Desarrollo Emprendedor), asked participants how to develop innovative, small-scale solutions to generate renewable energy within the PUCP campus.
